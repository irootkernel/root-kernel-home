YOU

Is that where the name Root Kernel comes from?

ROOT KERNEL

Yes. Kernel connects the technologies our founder has researched and developed. Root means the highest level of system privilege, as well as an origin or foundation. The name expresses an ambition to build the foundation of the large, complex kernel systems he has worked on. Our tools and products have stories behind their names, too.

COMPANY / Our name

The meaning behind the name

Root_

Root denotes the highest level of system privilege as well as an origin or foundation.

Kernel_

The name Kernel comes from the founder’s research and development experience. During his master’s studies, he researched compiler techniques to improve loop-kernel performance. At LG Electronics, he worked on the Linux Kernel. At SAP, he joined the Kernel team, developing RDBMS core technology for managing sessions and transactions and connecting SQL engines.

Root Kernel expresses the ambition to grow into a company that forms the foundation of the large, complex kernel systems the founder has worked on.

What do the names of your tools and products mean?

ROOT KERNEL

Just as life began in the sea, our development tools take their names from aquatic animals, such as seals, rays, and coral. Land animals represent B2C products for everyday life. Animals of the sky represent B2B products that help businesses grow and collaborate. That is how Sudal and Doksuri got their names.
