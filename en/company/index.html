Why is the company called Root Kernel?

What kind of person is the founder?

ROOT KERNEL

Yeonghun Jeong holds a master's degree in Computer Engineering and has been building software for more than 15 years. During his master's studies he researched kernel performance for GPUs and other parallel processors. At LG Electronics he worked on Linux Kernel, mobile OS, and ADAS projects. At SAP he did research and development on Kernel and Cloud DBaaS. He has earned CKA and CKAD certifications, and has a wide range of experience from CLI tools to enterprise services.
