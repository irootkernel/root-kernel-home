Company

Root Kernel is a one-person technology company building products, AI control technology, and open developer tools. Founded in June 2026, it works with AI throughout research and development.

Products expose real operating problems. Core technology generalizes the solution, frameworks and tools make it repeatable, and the result returns to the products. AI-SPARK and Podway form the control core of this cycle. Products, AI Harness, and the agent execution layer apply and test the principles at different layers, with Podway also published as part of AI Harness.

The name and its world

Root Kernel is both root and kernel. The name points to the deepest layer beneath the products, where boundaries are drawn between AI autonomy and human authority. AI-SPARK, Podway, and the Deterministic Core apply this principle in different execution environments.

Above it sit the deep sea of open developer tools, the land of Sudal's relationship-centered product, and the sky from which Doksuri surveys project flow. The names and visual world express the company's vertical structure from products down to foundational technology.

Founder

CEO Younghoon Jung is an engineer with 13 years of experience building mobile operating systems and database cores. At LG Electronics, he worked on kernel schedulers and power-management systems; at SAP Labs Korea, he developed SAP HANA's database core and cloud infrastructure.

Experience with kernel scheduling, failure recovery, and database consistency now informs the company's products and technology. Sudal and Doksuri preserve sessions and work across interruptions, Ember Quest reproduces execution through replays, and AI-SPARK binds validation and approval to a revision. An invention for session recovery is patented in the United States, Europe, and Japan.

Grants and patents

Ember Quest was selected for the 2026 Game Production Environment AI Transformation (AX) support program run by Korea's Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and the Korea Creative Content Agency. A core AI-SPARK invention has been filed as a Korean patent application.
