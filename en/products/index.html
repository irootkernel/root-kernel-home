Doksuri

IN DEVELOPMENT · CONCEPT PREVIEW

YOU

Doesn't that mean you end up talking only to AI?

ROOT KERNEL

Relationships between people matter to Root Kernel too. That is why we are building Sudal, a live would-you-rather game where people answer questions together and start conversations. Players share their choices and the reasons behind them, which keeps the conversation moving naturally.

02 / PEOPLE / CONVERSATION

Sudal

IN DEVELOPMENT · CONCEPT PREVIEW

A live would-you-rather game
that starts conversations

Sudal is a live would-you-rather game that helps friends, couples, and coworkers find things to talk about by answering questions together. Players choose their answers and explain why they made different choices, which keeps the conversation moving naturally.
