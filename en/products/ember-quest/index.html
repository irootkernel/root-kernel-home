Ember Quest — a physics puzzle RPG where embers open the way

Ember Quest is a portrait-mode pixel-art puzzle RPG in which you fire embers to light a dark labyrinth. Choose the direction and power of each shot, then use walls and reflectors to light braziers and open the door to the next floor.

Embers also reveal hidden spaces and help you face monsters. Choose angles and shot sequences with limited time and embers in mind, and find ways to reach several targets with a single shot.

Develop your hero as you descend through the labyrinth, using new skills to find your own solutions. Records left on each floor and the stories of its characters gradually reveal the labyrinth’s secrets.

Compete with other players through live rankings and replay past runs to revisit your choices. Enjoy trying again as you discover better routes and strategies.
